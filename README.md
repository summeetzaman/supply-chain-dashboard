# 📦 SupplyChain IQ • Inventory & Risk Dashboard

An interactive, web-based supply chain analytics and risk simulation tool designed to model inventory depletion, calculate critical reorder thresholds, and evaluate holding costs under market volatility. 

Built with **HTML5, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, and Chart.js**, this tool bridges operational MIS data structuring with real-world inventory management principles.

---

### 🚀 Key Features
* **Dynamic Reorder Point (ROP) & Safety Stock Calculation:** Instantly computes required inventory buffers based on average daily demand, lead times, and transit variance.
* **Interactive Parameter Sliders:** Real-time controls allowing supply chain managers to stress-test inventory policies under various demand and lead-time scenarios.
* **Automated Risk Assessment Engine:** Dynamically categorizes stockout risk levels (Optimized, Moderate, High Risk) with actionable operational insights.
* **Visual Depletion Modeling:** Integrates interactive line charts (powered by Chart.js) to visualize inventory burn-down cycles and restock timing.

---

### 📊 Mathematical Model
The dashboard applies standard inventory optimization formulas:
* **Safety Stock:** $\text{Demand} \times \text{Lead Time Variance Buffer}$
* **Reorder Point (ROP):** $(\text{Average Daily Demand} \times \text{Supplier Lead Time}) + \text{Safety Stock}$
* **Annual Holding Cost:** $\text{Average Inventory} \times \text{Carrying Cost per Unit}$
